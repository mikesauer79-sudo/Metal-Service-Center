Metal Service Center
====================

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars):
Reference data, calculators & nesting tools for metal service center professionals

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Metal Service Center is the essential pocket reference for metal service center professionals, fabricators, and structural engineers. Whether you're on the shop floor, in the yard, or at a customer site, everything you need is right in your pocket — and it works completely offline.

REFERENCE DATA
Browse and search a comprehensive library of material specifications:
• Material Grades — ASTM and AISI grades including A36, A572, A514, 4140, 4340, 304 and 316 stainless, and more, with full specification details
• Material Shape Data — weight per foot and per meter for structural shapes: wide flange, channel, angle, tube, pipe, bar, and plate
• Gauge Tables — sheet metal gauges (MSG) and tube gauges (BWG) with decimal and millimeter equivalents
• Aluminum Sheet — decimal thickness reference for aluminum sheet with lb/ft² and mm columns
• Tolerances — ASTM A6 dimensional tolerances for structural shapes
• Hardness Charts — full conversion table across Brinell (HB), Rockwell C (HRC), Rockwell B (HRB), Vickers (HV), and tensile strength
• Shape Dimensions — common structural shape dimensions with cross-section diagrams

CALCULATORS
Fast, accurate calculations based on standard AISC and ASTM formulas:
• Shape Weight — calculate weight for any structural shape by length and quantity
• Plate Weight — length × width × thickness weight calculation
• Bar / Rod Weight — round, square, and hex bar weight
• Coil Calculator — estimate coil weight, strip length, and outside diameter
• Bend Allowance — K-factor bend allowance, setback, and flat blank length
• Tonnage Calculator — press brake tonnage for 11 material types using the standard air-bend formula (T = C × t² × L / V), with V-die opening presets, minimum bend radius, minimum flange length, and setup warnings
• Yield / Utilization — material efficiency and remnant waste calculation
• Unit Conversion — imperial to metric and metric to imperial for length, weight, area, and pressure

NESTING OPTIMIZATION
Reduce material waste with on-device nesting algorithms:
• 1D Bar & Tube Nesting — optimize cut lists against one or more stock lengths using a first-fit-decreasing algorithm with kerf allowance; save and reload jobs
• 2D Plate Nesting — fit rectangular parts onto flat plate or sheet stock using shelf-packing with a visual layout diagram showing part placement

BUILT FOR THE JOB SITE
• Works 100% offline — no internet connection required, ever
• No account or login required
• No ads
• Fast and responsive — all data and calculations run on-device
• Portrait-optimized for one-handed use
